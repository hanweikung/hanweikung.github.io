I am a postdoctoral researcher at the [Multimedia and Human Understanding Group](https://mhug.disi.unitn.it), [University of Trento](https://www.unitn.it/en). My research focuses on AI-generated human faces under the guidance of [Nicu Sebe](https://disi.unitn.it/~sebe/). Previously, I worked at the [National University of Singapore](https://nus.edu.sg) with [Terence Sim](https://www.comp.nus.edu.sg/cs/people/tsim/) and as a software engineer at [Pinscreen](https://www.pinscreen.com) in Los Angeles. I completed my Ph.D. in [Media Arts and Technology](https://www.mat.ucsb.edu) at the [University of California, Santa Barbara](https://www.ucsb.edu), under the supervision of [Curtis Roads](https://www.curtisroads.net).
